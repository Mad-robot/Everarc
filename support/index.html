Reminders

My reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Everarc in the phone's settings, and that a Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't holding them. Settings → Reminder health in the app sends a test reminder and shows what it finds.

On Android, also allow "Alarms & reminders" for Everarc, and set its battery use to Unrestricted. Some phones close apps in the background to save battery, which can stop reminders.

Can a reminder ring like an alarm?

Yes. In a routine's Reminders, choose Alarm. On iPhone this uses the system alarm on iOS 26 and later; on Android it uses an alarm-strength notification.

Why did my reminders go quiet?

If Everarc hasn't been opened for 10 days, everyday reminders pause so they don't nag. Medicines and alarms keep going. Opening the app turns everything back on.

Your data

How do I back up?

Settings → Backup & moving. Make a backup file protected with a password you choose, and keep it wherever you like, or turn on Back up automatically so an encrypted copy rides along in the phone's own backup. Keep the password safe: without it, nobody (including us) can open the file.

How do I move to a new phone?

Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi. On the new phone, tap "Moving from another phone?" when you start; on the old phone, open Settings → Backup & moving → Move to a new phone and scan the code. Everything goes straight between the two phones. On iPhone, allow Local Network when asked.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a routine or photo in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Everarc has no account or server, so there is nothing stored anywhere else.

Everarc Plus

What does Plus add?

Everything works for free with up to 5 routines. Plus removes the limit, once for life or monthly.

I bought Plus on another phone

Open the Plus page and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. It stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Medicines

Everarc reminds you and keeps count; it doesn't give medical advice. Timing notes such as "before food" come from label information or from you. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's advice.
